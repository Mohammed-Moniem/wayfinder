# Code of Conduct

We want technical disagreement to be direct, useful, and respectful.

- Discuss behavior, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information, or use sexualized conduct.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and community health.

Report conduct concerns through a private repository channel. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or restrict contributions and participation when needed to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure.
